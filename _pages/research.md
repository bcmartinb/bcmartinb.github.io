## SpikeWise
### Action potential waveforms are state-dependent

![SpikeWise schematic](/assets/images/research/spikewise.png)

Action potentials are assumed to be binary in systems and cognitive neuroscience: voltage waveforms reduced to a digital "1." This constrains neural codes to rate, temporal, and population signaling. **SpikeWise** is a package we developed to parameterize spikes directly: waveform shape is not random but **state dependent**, varying systematically with brain state (input and LFP) and influencing the shape and timing of the next spike. This points to a broader landscape of neural codes, where neurons communicate through waveform structure as well as timing. One manuscript is available as a preprint and under review, and a second is in preparation; presented at SfN, FENS, and CNS.

*Preprint: [Action potential waveforms are state-dependent](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.15.751814), bioRxiv (2026)*

## Neuronal timescales across development

![Timescale development schematic](/assets/images/research/timescale-development.png)

**Neuronal timescales**, the characteristic duration over which a circuit integrates information, are an organizing principle of brain function. Comparing **human cortical organoids** to **rat hippocampal cultures**, this work traces how timescales develop and shift across developmental stages. Presented at SfN and CNS.

*Paper: [Development of neuronal timescales in human cortical organoids and rat hippocampus dissociated cultures](https://scholar.google.com/citations?view_op=view_citation&hl=en&oe=ASCII&user=PNR9T_8AAAAJ&citation_for_view=PNR9T_8AAAAJ:IjCSPb-OGe4C), J Neurophysiol (2024)*

</div>

## Human brain organoid MEA analysis methods

![Brain organoid MEA schematic](/assets/images/research/organoid-mea.png)

Built **analysis methods and pipelines** for **multi-electrode array (MEA)** recordings, which capture functional network activity from **human cortical organoids and dissociated neural cultures**, quantifying spike frequency, burst structure, and network-level metrics over development. **Cortical organoids** are stem-cell-derived three-dimensional neural tissues that recapitulate aspects of early brain development.

Collaborated on multiple projects, including new **high-density MEA recording protocols** and **closed-loop systems** for organoids (manuscript in preparation), **electrical and optogenetic stimulation** protocols, **graphene-polymer nanofiber** organoid interfaces ([Biomaterials](https://scholar.google.com/citations?view_op=view_citation&hl=en&oe=ASCII&user=PNR9T_8AAAAJ&citation_for_view=PNR9T_8AAAAJ:Y0pCki6q_DkC)), **ketamine effects** in 3D human neuronal cultures ([Biological Psychiatry](https://scholar.google.com/citations?view_op=view_citation&hl=en&oe=ASCII&user=PNR9T_8AAAAJ&citation_for_view=PNR9T_8AAAAJ:zYLM7Y9cAGgC)), and the study of neurodevelopmental conditions, such as patient-derived organoids carrying a **MAOA/MAOB deletion** (manuscript in preparation).

</div>

## Computational analysis of in vivo circadian bioluminescence in freely behaving mice

Developed a non-invasive method for recording circadian **bioluminescence** from freely behaving mice using **PER2::LUCIFERASE reporters**, along with a **computational pipeline** for extracting rhythm parameters from the resulting time-series data, as an undergraduate researcher. Four peer-reviewed publications; presented at SRBR and SfN.
