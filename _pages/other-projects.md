## Substantiated
*Trace the claim to the evidence*

Health and longevity companies love to claim they are "science-backed." **Substantiated** is an index that checks whether their claims actually hold up: whether the papers they cite were done on people, at the right dose, for the right outcome, alongside their staff scientists, regulatory filings, and independent testing.
